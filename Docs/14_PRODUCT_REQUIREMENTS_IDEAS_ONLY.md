# Product Requirements — Ideas Only

This document records desired gameplay/product ideas without importing legacy technical design.

## Enterprise/world
Holdings, subsidiaries, business units, facilities, branches, assets, banking, finance, HR, contracts, markets, world geography/currencies/timezones.

## Transport
Aviation, maritime, road/logistics; routes, scheduling, departures/arrivals, cargo/passenger demand, maintenance, crews, facilities and high-density asset map.

## Finance/Treasury
Real ledger/accounting depth, cash/banks, transfers, cheques, incoming/outgoing payments, invoices, payroll, tax, financing, loans, FX, future LC/guarantees, proof documents/images and audit history.

## Manufacturing/retail
Generic products/SKUs/BOM/recipes, procurement, suppliers, inventory, warehouses, manufacturing lines/orders, quality, distribution, retail, warranty/service and R&D.

Future examples: car dealership/manufacturer, dairy manufacturer/seller, phone manufacturer/retailer.

## UX
Fast large lists/search/filter, modern management dashboards, smooth map, no freezes during date advancement/mass operations/save, clear diagnostics export for development/testing.

Every item must receive a new NEXORA design from first principles.
