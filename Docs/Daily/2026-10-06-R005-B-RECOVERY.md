`bManifestState` owns parsing the fixed 64-byte committed frames; `bRecoverStore`
precedes a resumed close, and `bAppendManifest` writes the next frame with O_APPEND.
Previously recovery ignored a suffix shorter than 64 bytes but left it on disk.
Appending after that suffix broke frame alignment at the next recovery. The
summary and carry also require rollback to the last committed manifest frame.

The repair truncates that ignored suffix to the parsed committed byte length and
syncs the manifest before summary/carry cleanup and directory sync. It rejects a
summary shorter than its committed length. Direct append rejects a manifest whose
length is not exactly `64 * segment`, so callers cannot bypass recovery and write
behind a torn suffix. A valid full frame with bad CRC/magic still fails closed.

Recovery estimate: at most one extra manifest truncation and fsync on a torn tail;
normal close does one file-length check, with no new per-asset allocation or UI
advance-path work. The patch does not claim power-loss durability from SIGKILL.
